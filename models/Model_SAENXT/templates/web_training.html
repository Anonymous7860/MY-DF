SAENXT Training Controls Reference

A practical guide to the controls in this web panel. Open a section below for what each group changes, when it is useful, and whether it requires restarting training.
Quick Start and Applying Changes
Start Training: Starts the trainer with the settings currently shown.
Apply Changes: Sends supported live changes to the active trainer. Controls marked Requires restart are loaded when the model or data pipeline is created and do not rebuild it while training is running.
Save: Queues a normal model save. Save & Exit: saves and closes training.
Architecture fields: Architecture, face type, resolution, AE dimensions, encoder dimensions, decoder dimensions, and mask dimensions are locked after the model is created. Batch size is also locked while training is active.
Architecture Choices
liae-udt: Shared-decoder LIAE with separate identity and destination-structure slots.
liae-sb: LIAE plus the global SB style-bypass route.
df-udt: DF wiring with separate source and destination decoders.
df-sb: DF plus the global SB style-bypass route.
nxt-gip: Pair-specific Geometry-Identity-Photometry shared decoder with direct LIAE encoder/decoder pretrain compatibility.
nxt-mgip: Multi-person DST GIP. Each DST frame supplies frozen ArcFace identity for reconstruction; swap and DFM export use only the fixed SRC route.
Loss Weights
L1 Weight: Straight per-pixel error averaged over the face mask, on top of the main SSIM-family loss. Keeps color, brightness, and skin tone exact so the swap matches the destination's tones; lower it late in training to let LPIPS/GAN hallucinate finer detail.
LPIPS Weight: Perceptual distance from a calibrated VGG16 network (masked LPIPS-v0.1; FP32, 384px input cap, Nyquist guard against 2px grid artifacts). Judges the face the way human vision does — structure, texture, identity cues — so it fixes results that are numerically close but look waxy or 'off'. Start at 0 and raise once basics converge.
Multi-Scale Decoder Supervision: Adds training-only reconstruction heads at intermediate decoder scales. Speeds coarse-to-fine convergence; existing weights and DFM export remain compatible.
Landmark: Runs a frozen differentiable 68-point FAN landmark detector on the prediction and matches its points to the target's. Keeps pose, expression, and facial geometry locked to the destination — eyes and mouth land in the right place.
FFT Power: Matches the global frequency spectrum (amplitude and phase). Recovers overall sharpness and texture that pixel losses blur away; 0.1=gentle, 1.0=strong, 5-10=late-stage sharpening.
FFT Detail Boost: Skews the FFT loss toward the highest frequencies — pores, wrinkles, teeth edges — when the face is stable but fine detail stays soft. 0.10=default.
Freq Band Detail: Splits the face into Laplacian frequency bands, matches each band, and rewards sharp detail. Improves local detail and micro-contrast without FFT's global bias. 0.5-2.0=gentle, 5.0+=aggressive.
Face Style: Applies destination face-style pressure to the swap face so its overall look (lighting, tone) blends with the destination scene.
Eye, Mouth, and Teeth Detail
Eye/Mouth Priority: Adds strong reconstruction pressure over the complete landmark eye and mouth regions.
Mouth Focus: Adds detail pressure across the full mouth and teeth interior when the entire mouth looks soft.
Mask-Free Dental Focus (experimental): Builds temporary target-derived upper and lower dental attention inside the landmark mouth region. It requires no saved teeth masks or preprocessing and skips samples without a confident dental-looking region.
Enable Teeth Mask Training: Enables the controls that use saved teeth masks. It does not remove normal whole-mouth training.
Teeth-aware Sampling: Preferentially samples aligned images that contain usable teeth masks.
Teeth Focus: Adds detail loss only inside the saved teeth mask.
Teeth L1 Focus: Adds teeth-only pixel pressure inside the saved mask.
Lower Teeth Boost: Biases the existing teeth losses toward the lower portion of the teeth mask.
Training Parameters and Precision
Target Iteration: Automatically stops at this iteration. Zero means manual stop.
Autobackup Hour / Max Backups: Controls scheduled backups and how many are retained. A maximum of zero means unlimited retention.
Loss Function: Selects SSIM, MS-SSIM, or the combined MS-SSIM+L1 reconstruction objective.
Optimizer: Selects Muon, AdaBelief, ACProp, or Adam.
Learning Rate: Controls optimizer step size. 5e-5 is the standard displayed default.
Mixed Precision: Off uses FP32; FP16 and BF16 reduce activation memory and can improve speed. Numerically sensitive loss and stability paths remain in FP32 where required.
Foreground Occlusion: Adds objects to DST inputs and removes their coverage from face-mask targets, teaching the learned masks to leave foreground objects visible. Range: 0.00-1.00. Zero disables it, 0.50 affects half of samples, and 1.00 affects every DST sample. Mask learning requires the learned mask branch. Requires a trainer restart.
Occlusion Images: Optional folder of transparent PNG or WebP foreground cutouts. Leave it empty to use procedural soft objects.
Swap and SB Identity Controls
Swap Align: LIAE and LIAE-SB only. Directly supervises the AB_dst swap path. Start around 0.05-0.20.
DF Swap Align: DF and DF-SB only. Trains decoder_src on destination-code swap structure using mask and edge supervision. Start around 0.05-0.15.
Swap BG: Trains the swap outside the destination face mask toward the DST background.
SB Recon BG: LIAE-SB only. Applies low-frequency outside-mask stabilization to the normal SRC and DST reconstructions. It is hot, defaults to 0.10, and is separate from Swap BG.
SB Style RGB: SB-only appearance blend toward a rolling SRC style bank. This is an appearance control, not a structural identity loss.
SB Identity Shift: Moves the swap style center toward SRC identity and detail while retaining the current DST frame's pose/expression style delta.
SB Source Identity Guidance: Selects the SRC-native SB style route for training, preview, and DFM export. Identity Strength adds frozen ArcFace relative SRC-versus-DST pressure.
NXT Training Options
Only used by the NXT architectures (nxt-gip / nxt-mgip); MGIP rows appear when the architecture is nxt-mgip. Source mask and morphology rows are GIP/MGIP only.

NXT Geometry Confusion: Reversed SRC/DST domain-classifier pressure on the geometry Inter. 0.10 is the default, 0.20 is aggressive, 0.30 is the maximum.
GIP Source Coverage Mask: Restart-scoped. Trains a small mask head only on SRC masks and changes only swap/DFM coverage; RGB and the native DST mask remain unchanged. The GIP Source Coverage slider is a hot swap-mask blend from the native NXT mask (0.00) to the SRC-trained coverage mask (1.00).
NXT DF-Style Source Morphology: Restart-scoped. Trains source-only spatial residuals at coarse and mid RGB decoder scales for more source nose, lip, cheek, jaw, and profile authority. The NXT Source Morphology slider hot-blends preview/swap/DFM output toward the trained branch while retaining DST pose and expression.
GIP LIAE Identity Split: Restart-scoped. RGB gets a shared SRC geometry branch and a DST-private reconstruction branch; once trained the option stays enabled for the model.
Source Material Tokens (MGIP): Restart-scoped identity route building a 4x4 EMA source-only material token bank applied only to SRC/swap RGB. Source Material Power: 0.00 is the recommended structural-only mode; above 0 adds optional ArcFace guidance (max 0.25).
DST-Conditioned Shared Slot (MGIP): Identity Split only, live-toggleable. Conditions the DST-reconstruction shared slot with the DST identity instead of the SRC token so DST-only content (hair, occluders) trains into the shared route the swap renders from.
MGIP Token Alignment: Restart-scoped copied-slot experiment adding a zero-init source-only channel adapter after FiLM; sparse ArcFace guidance updates only the source token and adapter. Token Alignment Power: 0.10 recommended; 0.00 pauses guidance while keeping the adapter active (max 0.25).
GAN and True Face
GAN Power: Patch-adversarial detail pressure. Zero disables it; 0.10-0.50 is the usual working range after the base reconstruction has become stable.
True Face: Latent-code discriminator pressure. Zero disables it; 0.01-0.10 is a gentle range.
Patch Size: Sets the discriminator receptive field. A common starting point is roughly training resolution divided by eight.
GAN Dims: Sets discriminator channel width. Higher values add capacity and VRAM use.
Smoothing: Softens discriminator labels to reduce overconfidence.
Noisy Labels: Randomly flips a small fraction of real/fake labels as regularization.
Color Transfer and Augmentation
Color Transfer: Choose none, classic transfer modes, FS-Aug, CC-Aug, D-Aug Lite, or D-Aug. D-Aug Lite keeps FS-Aug on SRC and applies milder asymmetric lighting changes to DST; D-Aug is the aggressive version.
Aug Mask Mode: Restricts supported augmentation to eyes, mouth, or both.
Random Warp: Original DFL facial deformation used for generalization.
Single-Pass Random Warp: Experimental faster RGB sampling path. Off keeps the original two-pass behavior. It only applies while Random Warp is enabled and requires restart.
Uniform Yaw: Balances sampling across yaw angles.
Random SRC / DST Flip: Independently mirrors source or destination samples.
Masked Training: Restricts reconstruction learning to the dataset or XSeg face mask. Blur Out Mask and Background Blur control non-face preprocessing.
Optimization
Training Priority: Windows scheduling priority for the trainer and loader workers. Idle is the legacy behavior; Normal can reduce GPU starvation. Requires restart.
CPU Cap: Total SRC plus DST data-loader worker processes, split evenly. The default of 8 means four SRC and four DST workers. Requires restart.
Thread Limit: Per-worker OpenMP, MKL, OpenBLAS, NumExpr, and OpenCV thread limit. Zero selects the automatic limit of two. Requires restart.
Faceset Cache: Keeps decoded faces in bounded system RAM to reduce repeated JPEG/PNG decoding. Cache MB is a per-worker budget. Requires restart.
Fused Sample Loading: Packs each worker batch contiguously and uploads SRC and DST once per side. Requires restart.
GPU Prefetch Queue: Keeps two pinned GPU-ready batches queued while the current batch trains. This reduces loader stalls but uses additional RAM and VRAM. Requires restart.
Channels Last: Uses NVIDIA channels-last tensor layout for supported convolution paths. VRAM use can differ slightly. Requires restart.
Encoder / Decoder Grad Checkpoint: Trades extra recomputation for lower activation VRAM.
CPU Offload Optimizer: Moves optimizer state away from VRAM at the cost of CPU/RAM traffic.
Activation CPU Offload: Stores tensors needed for backward in pinned system RAM and restores them without changing values or dtype. It can substantially lower peak VRAM, but PCIe transfers slow training. Safe to toggle live.
CUDA Benchmark: Lets cuDNN tune convolution algorithms for fixed shapes.
Rank-32 RGB Latent Adapter: LIAE-only zero-start residual latent adapter. It begins as an exact identity for pretrained weights; once trained, keep it enabled. Requires restart.
Gradient Accumulation, Stability, and Anti-Flicker
Gradient Accumulation: Splits one batch into micro-batches and performs one optimizer update after all pieces. Batch size must be divisible by the selected steps. It lowers activation VRAM with some speed cost.
Gradient Clipping: Limits gradient norm before optimizer update. Clip Norm controls the limit.
LR Dropout: Randomly freezes the selected percentage of weights for each update to reduce late-training subpixel shake.
Anti-Flicker: Late-training camera/detector-jitter simulation. Translation, scale, and rotation control the small perturbation range. It is normally used after Random Warp is disabled.
Layer Freezing: Freezes the selected encoder, Inter_B, or decoder stage immediately or at the specified iteration.
Learned Mask Branch and XSeg-Only Merge
Train Learned Mask Branch on: Builds and trains the model mask decoder. This is required for learned-mask merge modes and DFM export.
Off: No mask-decoder parameters, forward compute, mask loss, mask gradients, or mask optimizer state are created. RGB loss still uses the aligned dataset mask or saved XSeg mask.
In XSeg-only mode, use merger mask mode 6 (XSeg-prd), 7 (XSeg-dst), or 8 (XSeg-prd * XSeg-dst). Learned-mask merge modes and DFM export are unavailable.
Re-enabling before training starts preserves the current RGB checkpoint. If the mask branch was trained before being disabled, its weights and compatible optimizer state resume from the latest pre-XSeg backup. If it was disabled from the beginning, it starts fresh at the saved Mask Dims.
Experimental Research Features
These controls are experimental and default to Off. Make a backup and A/B test exported results. Loss-only controls can be changed live. Paired-Domain Fusion requires a trainer restart. STAM, Source Identity Bridge, and Multi-Scale AdaIN+ add checkpoint parameters, require a trainer restart, and remain enabled after their new weights have trained.

Paired-Domain Fusion: Combines SRC and DST into one larger batch for the shared encoder and shared DF Inter or LIAE Inter_AB, then combines compatible reconstruction-loss calls. Decoder branches remain separate because SRC, DST, and swap do not always have identical semantics. It does not add parameters, alter checkpoint structure, or change DFM export. It preserves the same objective, although floating-point kernel and reduction order can produce small rounding differences. The larger paired operations can improve GPU efficiency when launch overhead or small per-domain workloads limit speed, while transient activation workspace may rise. Requires a trainer restart.

Composite Mask Loss: Replaces the learned mask's plain MSE objective with the same MSE plus soft Dice overlap and boundary matching. This directly pressures the complete mask shape and contour. It adds no export layer and requires the learned mask branch. Stronger contour pressure can be counterproductive when the training masks are inaccurate.

Semantic Mask Head: Adds a training-only three-class auxiliary prediction for visible face, foreground occluder, and background. Foreground Occlusion supplies the occluder target; without it, the head mainly learns face versus background. Its cross-entropy and Dice loss train mask features, while the normal one-channel learned mask, merger contract, and DFM output remain unchanged. It requires the learned mask branch.

Multi-Scale GAN: Reuses the same patch discriminator at full, half, and quarter resolution and averages the three adversarial objectives; it does not create three independent discriminators. Fine texture is judged at full scale and broader structure at reduced scales. It only acts when GAN Power is above zero and increases training compute.

Temporal / Transform Consistency: This is synthetic transform-equivariance training, not true neighboring-frame or optical-flow training. One DST sample per micro-batch receives a second swap pass with a tiny random transform: up to about 1.5 px translation, 1% scale, and 0.5 degree rotation. RGB and learned-mask output are trained to follow that transform. It targets small alignment jitter and flicker but adds another encoder/swap-decoder pass.
Temporal Weight: Scales the consistency objective. 0.10 is the default starting value; higher values impose stronger transform stability.

STAM Depth: Adds parallel channel and spatial attention to RGB decoder features. Coarse Stage inserts one block after the first coarse decoder block; Coarse + Mid adds another at mid resolution. Zero-start residual gates preserve the old output when first enabled. Greater depth adds parameters, VRAM, and compute, and trained depth cannot later be reduced in the GUI.

Source Identity Bridge: LIAE only. Source reconstruction maintains a moving 4x4 source-token bank, and source/swap RGB decoder features cross-attend to it. Its residual gate starts at zero and the trained bridge is included in DFM swap inference. It aims to reinforce source-specific structure but can over-constrain expression or destination geometry if it becomes too strong.

Multi-Scale AdaIN+: SB architectures only. Learns a separate zero-gated style transform for every decoder scale, including enabled resolution-growth stages, then sends scale-specific style to each stage's AdaIN. It can distribute identity, color, and texture style more precisely, but can also exaggerate style transfer. Judge it using exported swaps, not training loss alone.

Compatibility: Paired-Domain Fusion, Composite Mask, Semantic Mask, Multi-Scale GAN, and Temporal Consistency do not change the exported DFM interface. STAM, Identity Bridge, and Multi-Scale AdaIN+ become part of the trained decoder/export path and must remain enabled when resuming or exporting that checkpoint.
Resolution Growth
Resolution Growth changes the active training resolution in stages while retaining compatible learned weights. Stage 0 is the base resolution; negative stages are lower-resolution pretraining; positive stages are higher-resolution detail stages.

Before training, the selected stage is used at startup. While training is active, use Transition Stage While Running to move upward. Transitions are one-way within the active checkpoint. LR Decay can slow inner rings during a progressive transition.
Decoder / Mask Capacity Growth
Growth Mode: Widens Decoder + Mask together, Decoder only, or Mask only.
Maximum Built Stage: Highest capacity stage available in the checkpoint; it does not activate that stage by itself.
Capacity Stage: Selects the width currently in use.
Training Scope: Chooses whether only the current stage, current plus previous, all active, or newly direct-expanded stages can update.
Direct Expansion: Jumps over intermediate stages while honoring Training Scope. Use Direct-expansion stages to train only newly added stages, or All Active Stages to train the complete active width immediately. Sequential growth uses the least VRAM.
Preview, Metrics, and Debug Controls
Zoom Preview: Adds a closer preview view. Show Filenames: identifies sampled faces.
Seam / Mask Edge: overlays the mask boundary to reveal leaks and poor seam placement.
Latent / Bottleneck: adds encoder, Inter, bottleneck, decoder-input, and decoded-output diagnostic views.
Training Metrics Help: The separate Help button inside the metrics panel explains how to interpret every chart.
VRAM Debug / Quality Debug: enable their respective diagnostic collection.
Crash Reports: optional fatal, exception, and non-finite reporting plus generator-gradient monitoring. Leave it off when you do not need incident reporting.

How to Read Training Metrics

These panels auto-scale per chart, so compare a chart against its own past behavior instead of comparing the slope of different charts to each other. Some charts only appear when the related feature is enabled.
Quick Rules
SRC / DST / Both: SRC charts are source reconstruction, DST charts are destination reconstruction, and Both overlays the two together.
Most loss charts: Lower is better. A healthy chart usually trends downward over time, even if it wiggles.
Similarity / ratio charts: Higher is better for SSIM, MS-SSIM, and MaskIoU. HF is best when it moves toward 1.0.
Do not compare chart heights across panels: Every chart uses its own scale.
Core Reconstruction Charts
Reconstruction: Main image-fit error. Lower is better. If this keeps falling but the face still looks waxy, the model may be learning shape before detail.
Total: Full weighted loss for that branch. Lower is better, but it can stay noisy if many optional losses are active.
L1: Raw pixel difference. Lower is better. Helpful for checking whether mouth, eye, and skin placement are actually matching.
Landmark: Pose and facial-point alignment. Lower is better. If it stays high, expressions and mouth shapes often lag.
Structure And Sharpness Charts
SSIM / MS-SSIM: Higher is better. These show whether facial structure is lining up even if texture is still soft.
FFT: Frequency-domain mismatch. Lower is better. If it stays high, the model is missing the right detail density.
FreqBand: High-frequency band loss. Lower is better. One of the most useful charts for teeth, pores, lips, and crisp edges.
HF: Best when it moves toward 1.0. Below 1.0 usually means predictions are too smooth; well above 1.0 can mean oversharpening or noisy detail.
SpatialConsistency: Lower is better. If it rises while other losses improve, the image may be getting patchy or unstable.
Mask And Color Charts
Mask: Predicted mask error. Lower is better. If this stays high, masked previews can show holes or broken cutouts even when RGB reconstruction looks okay.
MaskIoU: Higher is better. Closer to 1.0 means cleaner overlap between predicted and target masks.
ColorMatch: Color-distribution distance. Lower is better. Helpful for spotting color drift between prediction and target.
LPIPS: Perceptual difference. Lower is better. If it plateaus high while L1 improves, the face may still look perceptually wrong.
MultiScaleDecoder: Weighted reconstruction from the two training-only intermediate decoder heads. Lower is better.
Training-State Charts
GradNorm_G: Generator gradient strength. There is no perfect target number. You mainly want it stable. Sudden spikes often mean instability; a flat near-zero line can mean learning has stalled.
LR: Current learning rate. Not a quality score.
LR_Effective: Effective LR after warmup, cosine schedule, or runtime modifiers.
LR_Dropout: The dropout multiplier itself. Lower means more aggressive LR dropout behavior.
LR_Dropout_ON: Binary state chart. 1 means LR dropout is active, 0 means it is off.
SwapMaskIoU / Drift / AreaRatio: DFM mask agreement diagnostics. These appear automatically when the swap forward path is available. IoU closer to 1.0, Drift closer to 0.0, and AreaRatio near 1.0 mean the swap mask is staying aligned with the DST recon mask. AreaRatio below 1.0 means the swap mask is smaller.
Special Feature Charts
SwapAlign_Total / Mask / CodeAlign: Lower is better. These only appear when Swap Align is enabled and show how hard swap-specific supervision is still pushing.
GAN_D_loss: Combined real-plus-fake discriminator BCE. Near 1.386 is the neutral 50/50 point, but use the separate real/fake scores to interpret it.
GAN_G_loss: Unweighted per-image generator adversarial BCE. Lower means the current discriminator is treating generated faces as more real; it is expected to oscillate.
GAN_D_real: Mean discriminator probability for real SRC training faces (0-1). Higher means real faces are being recognized as real.
GAN_D_fake: Mean discriminator probability for generated SRC reconstructions (0-1). Lower means the discriminator is spotting fakes; rising toward D_real means the generator is fooling it more often.
AutoReset_WindowAvg: Rolling average DST loss used by Auto Inter Reset. Lower is better.
AutoReset_Stagnation: Counts how many consecutive windows have not improved enough. Rising toward your patience setting means a reset may trigger.
AutoReset_Triggered: Spikes to 1 when a reset actually happens.
What Good And Bad Looks Like
Healthy pattern: Reconstruction and Total trend down over time, SSIM or MS-SSIM trend up, MaskIoU trends up, and GradNorm_G stays active without wild spikes.

Warning signs: SRC improving much slower than DST, FreqBand and FFT refusing to budge, Mask loss staying high with MaskIoU flat, or GradNorm_G repeatedly spiking and crashing.

For detail problems like teeth or lips: Watch Reconstruction, L1, FreqBand, HF, and Mask together. If shape losses improve but FreqBand stays high and HF stays well below 1.0, the model is usually still too smooth.
